✨ Features

🎞
Audio & Video Merge
Combine any video with a separate audio track into a single MP4.
🚀
Blazing Fast
Video stream is copied without re-encoding, so it finishes in seconds.
🔒
Fully Local
Everything runs on your device. No files are ever uploaded.
🕒
Timestamped Output
Each result gets a unique name, so nothing is ever overwritten.
📱
Gallery Integration
Merged videos show up in your gallery automatically.
🔄
Auto Update
Built-in update checker keeps you on the latest version.

📖 How to Use

  1. Open BiliXia and tap Select Video to pick a video file.
  2. Tap Select Audio to pick the audio track you want to add.
  3. Tap Start Merging and wait for the progress bar to finish.
  4. Find your result in the gallery, or browse to Movies/BXia/.
